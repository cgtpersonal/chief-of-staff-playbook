# Weekly Priority Dashboard Template

## Purpose
Give leadership a single, clear view of what matters this week — priorities, status, blockers — so nothing important slips and decisions can be made fast.

## Dashboard structure

### 1. Top priorities (max 5)
| Priority | Owner | Status | Due | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | 🟢 On track / 🟡 At risk / 🔴 Blocked | | |

### 2. Bottlenecks & decisions needed
- What's blocked, who owns unblocking it, what decision or input is needed from leadership

### 3. Financial snapshot
- Cash position summary
- Overdue receivables (link to collections tracker)
- Upcoming payment obligations

### 4. People snapshot
- Open roles and pipeline stage
- Onboarding/offboarding in progress
- Any employee relations items requiring attention

### 5. Wins & closed items
- What got fully resolved this week

## Weekly cadence
- **Monday:** compile dashboard from previous week's trackers (finance, hiring, ops)
- **Before leadership check-in:** review with CEO/founder, flag anything needing a decision
- **Friday:** update status on all open items, carry forward what's still in progress

## Escalation principle
Anything that's been "at risk" for 2+ consecutive weeks moves to the top of the priorities list and gets a dedicated conversation — it doesn't just carry forward silently.
