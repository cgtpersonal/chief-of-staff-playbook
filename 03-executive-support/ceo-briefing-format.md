# CEO/Founder Briefing Format

## Purpose
A consistent format for briefing senior leadership — quick to prepare, quick to read, and structured so decisions can be made without digging for context.

## Format

### 1. Headline (1 sentence)
The single most important thing leadership needs to know right now.

### 2. Context (2–3 sentences)
Just enough background to understand the headline — no history lesson.

### 3. Current status
- What's done
- What's in progress
- What's blocked

### 4. Decision or input needed
- Be explicit: "I need you to decide X by [date]" or "FYI only, no action needed"
- Provide 2–3 options if a decision is required, with a recommendation

### 5. Risks & watch-items
- Anything that could escalate if not addressed

### 6. Next steps
- Who owns what, by when

## Delivery principles
- Lead with the headline — never bury it
- One page maximum for a written briefing
- Verbal briefings: 2 minutes max unless asked to go deeper
- Always separate fact from recommendation — be clear about which is which
- When flagging a problem, always bring at least one possible path forward

## Common formats this applies to
- Weekly 1:1 briefing
- Ad hoc urgent escalation
- Monthly ops/finance summary
- Pre-meeting prep note
