# Case Example: Cutting Time-to-Fill from 60 to 11.9 Days

## Context
At M Group Residential, full-cycle recruiting across multiple residential properties in Texas was slow and manual — sourcing, screening, interviewing, and offer management had no standardized system, and average time-to-fill sat around 60 days.

## What I built

### Systems
- Implemented **Workable ATS** and **GoHighLevel CRM** for recruiting pipelines, including automations, bulk actions, and candidate tracking
- Integrated with **Prism HRIS** for seamless data flow from hire to system-of-record
- Deployed a custom **ChatGPT-based recruiting agent** ("MGR Recruiting") to handle first-pass candidate interaction
- Added **Aptive** for candidate assessment and **1-way video interviewing**, both integrated directly into the ATS
- Standardized SOPs and playbooks for the full recruiting cycle

### Process changes
- Removed manual handoffs between sourcing, screening, and scheduling by automating pipeline stage transitions
- Built KPI dashboards tracking time-to-fill, pipeline health, retention, headcount, and turnover, reported directly to ownership
- Used Elise AI for candidate follow-up and team performance metrics

## Result
- Average time-to-fill dropped from **60 days to 11.9 days**
- Increased hiring manager satisfaction through standardized, predictable process
- Reduced manual follow-up load across the recruiting team

## Why it worked
The bottleneck wasn't sourcing volume — it was manual coordination between tools and people. Automating pipeline handoffs and giving hiring managers a single source of truth removed the friction that was adding weeks to every hire.
