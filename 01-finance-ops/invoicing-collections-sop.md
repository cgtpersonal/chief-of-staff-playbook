# Invoicing & Collections SOP

## Purpose
Ensure invoices go out accurately and on time, and that overdue payments are followed up on consistently — without letting cash flow risk go unnoticed.

## Cadence
- **Invoicing:** issued within 24–48h of the billing trigger (month-end, milestone, or contract date)
- **Collections check:** every Monday, review full receivables tracker
- **Escalation:** any invoice 15+ days overdue gets a direct follow-up; 30+ days gets flagged to leadership

## Process

### 1. Invoice issuance
- Confirm amount, client billing details, and payment terms against the signed contract
- Generate invoice (accounting tool of choice: QuickBooks / Xero / Zoho Books)
- Send with clear due date and payment instructions
- Log in receivables tracker: client, amount, issue date, due date, status

### 2. Tracking
- Maintain a single source-of-truth tracker (columns: Client | Invoice # | Amount | Issue Date | Due Date | Status | Last Follow-up | Notes)
- Update status weekly: *Pending / Overdue / Paid / Disputed*

### 3. Follow-up cadence
| Days overdue | Action |
|---|---|
| 1–14 | Friendly reminder email |
| 15–29 | Direct follow-up call/message, confirm expected payment date |
| 30+ | Escalate internally, flag as cash-flow risk |

### 4. Reconciliation
- Match incoming payments to invoices weekly
- Flag discrepancies same-day
- Close the loop: mark invoice paid, archive documentation

## Risk flags to escalate immediately
- Client disputes an invoice amount
- Payment pattern shifts (previously on-time client now late)
- Any single overdue invoice exceeds [X]% of monthly revenue

## Tools referenced
QuickBooks · Xero · Zoho Books · Google Sheets tracker
