# Vendor Payments & Cash-Flow Tracker

## Purpose
Keep recurring payments, subscriptions, and vendor obligations organized and predictable — so nothing is missed and cash-flow risk is visible before it becomes a problem.

## Tracker structure

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| Vendor/Service | Name of vendor or subscription |
| Category | Payroll / Software / Contractor / Utilities / Other |
| Amount | Recurring amount (flag if variable) |
| Frequency | Monthly / Quarterly / Annual |
| Due Date | Fixed day of month or renewal date |
| Payment Method | Bank transfer / card / other |
| Status | Scheduled / Paid / Overdue / Under Review |
| Owner | Who approves/executes the payment |
| Notes | Contract end date, auto-renewal flags, etc. |

## Monthly cadence

### Week 1
- Pull full list of upcoming payments for the month
- Confirm funds availability against payment obligations
- Flag any gap between expected cash and required outflows

### Ongoing
- Execute payments on scheduled due dates
- Log confirmation/receipt for each payment
- Update tracker status same-day

### Month-end
- Reconcile tracker against bank/accounting records
- Review upcoming subscription renewals — cancel or renegotiate anything no longer needed
- Prepare short cash-flow summary for leadership: obligations met, upcoming risks, any payment gaps

## Payroll coordination
- Confirm headcount and any changes (new hires, terminations, rate changes) before each cycle
- Cross-check hours/commissions/bonuses with source data
- Submit for processing with enough lead time for review
- Confirm payment executed and payslips distributed

## Cash-flow risk flags — escalate immediately
- Projected outflows exceed available funds in the next 2 weeks
- A recurring payment is missed or delayed
- Unexpected large expense outside the tracker
- Client receivables significantly behind schedule (see `invoicing-collections-sop.md`)
