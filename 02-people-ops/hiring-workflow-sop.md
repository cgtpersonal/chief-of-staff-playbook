# Hiring Workflow SOP

## Purpose
Standardize the hiring process end-to-end — from job posting to offer — so pipelines move fast, candidates get a consistent experience, and hiring managers always know where things stand.

## Process overview

### 1. Intake
- Confirm role, level, compensation band, and must-have requirements with hiring manager
- Finalize job description and posting channels

### 2. Sourcing & screening
- Post to relevant channels (LinkedIn, Indeed, niche boards depending on role)
- Screen inbound applications against must-haves
- Initial phone/video screen: confirm fit, availability, compensation expectations

### 3. Interview loop
- Schedule interviews with hiring manager/team
- Provide interviewers with a scorecard aligned to role competencies
- Collect structured feedback within 24h of each interview

### 4. Candidate follow-up
- Update candidate on status within 48h of any stage change
- No candidate left without a response for more than 5 business days

### 5. Offer stage
- Align internally on offer terms before extending
- Extend offer with clear deadline
- Track acceptance/negotiation/decline
- Send rejection to other finalists once role is filled

### 6. Handoff to onboarding
- Once signed, trigger onboarding process (see `employee-lifecycle-framework` repo)

## Pipeline tracker fields
Candidate | Role | Stage | Source | Screen Date | Interview Date(s) | Feedback | Offer Status | Owner | Notes

## KPIs to track
- Time-to-fill (days from posting to offer accepted)
- Pipeline conversion by stage
- Source effectiveness
- Hiring manager satisfaction
