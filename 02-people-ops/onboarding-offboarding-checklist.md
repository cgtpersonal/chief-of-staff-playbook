# Onboarding & Offboarding Checklist

## Purpose
Ensure every employee transition — in or out — is handled with proper documentation, access management, and communication, with nothing falling through the cracks.

## Onboarding

### Before Day 1
- [ ] Offer letter/contract signed
- [ ] Compensation and start date confirmed in HRIS
- [ ] Equipment ordered/assigned
- [ ] Accounts and system access provisioned (email, tools, ATS/HRIS, Slack, etc.)
- [ ] Manager and team notified of start date
- [ ] Welcome email sent with first-day logistics

### Day 1
- [ ] Welcome/orientation session
- [ ] Handbook and policies shared
- [ ] Benefits enrollment initiated (if applicable)
- [ ] Introductions to team and key stakeholders
- [ ] Access to all required systems confirmed working

### First 30/60/90 days
- [ ] 30-day check-in: role clarity, early blockers
- [ ] Training/role-specific onboarding completed
- [ ] 60-day check-in: performance expectations review
- [ ] 90-day check-in: formal review, confirm fit and next goals

## Offboarding

### Upon notice/decision
- [ ] Confirm last working day and offboarding type (voluntary/involuntary)
- [ ] Notify relevant stakeholders (manager, payroll, IT) on a need-to-know basis
- [ ] Prepare final pay/benefits documentation

### Before last day
- [ ] Knowledge transfer plan in place
- [ ] Company property return arranged (equipment, badges, etc.)
- [ ] Exit interview scheduled

### Last day
- [ ] All system access revoked
- [ ] Final pay and benefits processed
- [ ] Exit interview conducted and documented
- [ ] Internal announcement to team (as appropriate)

### Post-departure
- [ ] Records archived per policy
- [ ] Any rehire eligibility noted
